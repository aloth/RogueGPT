RogueGPT is a modular, model-agnostic framework for generating diverse, reproducible AI news stimuli under controlled experimental conditions - connecting static text generation with agent-based simulation of misinformation diffusion.

From static generation to agentic campaigns

An extensible architecture across LLM families, languages, styles and formats.

🤖

Multi-Model

Model-agnostic across GPT-4, LLaMA, Mistral and DeepSeek - not locked to a single generator like GROVER.

🌍

Multilingual & Controllable

Generate across languages, journalistic styles and content formats with parameter-rich control.

🕸️

Agent-Based Simulation

Standardizes the "seeding" phase of misinformation diffusion models for reproducible experiments.

🔗

Full Provenance

Every stimulus is tracked end-to-end, linking generation to human-perception data in JudgeGPT.

Built for four communities: misinformation researchers needing controlled stimuli, red-teamers stress-testing detectors, agent-based simulation researchers needing reproducible seeds, and human-factors researchers studying perception.
